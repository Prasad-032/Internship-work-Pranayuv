The AYUV Hybrid Bed – Mobility Care Edition is a next-generation smart hospital bed designed to address patient care for immobile and mobility-impaired patients. It integrates hygiene management, safety monitoring, and smart control into a single system — engineered to reduce caregiver workload and eliminate manual patient transfers.
